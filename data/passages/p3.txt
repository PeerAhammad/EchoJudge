Arjun left home early because he wanted to reach the railway station before the morning rush. The sky was clear, and he decided to walk instead of taking a bus. Halfway through the journey, dark clouds gathered, and heavy rain began to fall. He took shelter beneath a small shop awning and waited for the rain to slow down. A shopkeeper offered him an old umbrella that someone had left behind. Arjun accepted it gratefully, reached the station on time, and promised to return the umbrella the next day.
