On Monday afternoon, Ananya discovered that her notebook was missing just before class began. She checked her backpack, searched beneath the desk, and asked her classmates if anyone had seen it. Nobody knew where it was. After the lesson, she returned to the library where she had studied earlier. She found the notebook beside a window, partly hidden behind a stack of books. Ananya thanked the librarian for keeping it safe and hurried back to class. Although she had lost valuable time, she was relieved that her notes were still there.
