One morning, Rahul was walking to college when he found a wallet near a bus stop. He picked it up and opened it carefully. Inside, there was some money and an identity card. Rahul looked around, but nobody seemed to be searching for anything. He decided to call the phone number printed on the card. A few minutes later, an old man arrived. He looked relieved when Rahul returned the wallet. The man thanked Rahul and smiled. Rahul continued walking to college, feeling happy that he had made the right decision.
